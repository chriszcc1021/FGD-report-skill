1Project background

This report is based on the compilation of Gold & Glory / GnG core combat FGD materials in May 2026, covering the three markets of Mexico, Vietnam, and Indonesia.
The focus is on the 3C combat experience of the new version: camera perspective, operation control, dodge, enemy lock/auto-aim, weapon block, professional combat positioning, HUD and combat information.
PvE, theme art, sound, social, and new character concepts serve as supplemental observations.

  • new player: First contact feedback from VN Group 1, ID Group 3 New User, and MX Shooting to reference player materials to observe the appeal, onboarding barriers, and information comprehension costs of the new version 3C.
  • Hardcore veteran gamer: MX KOL/veteran player group, VN Group 3, ID Group 1 Hardcore, used to observe whether the legacy version's feel, operation limit, dungeon pressure, professional weaknesses and construction depth are retained.
  • Losing veteran players: VN Group 2 veteran players/churned players, ID Group 2 Churned, and MX veteran players return-related feedback, used to observe whether the new version can attract players again, and whether the main churn obstacles have been repaired.

2Core Conclusions

GnG's new version of 3C has clear appeal to new users. Players recognize the dark fantasy/medieval theme, 3v3 team collaboration, professional division of labor, skill performance and partial combat feedback. New players are more likely to be attracted by "it looks good, is easy to use, and can be played with friends"; veteran players and core players are more concerned about whether the dungeon tension, operation limit, professional weaknesses, and equipment/construction depth of the legacy version are still there.

New players are mainly stuck by "unsmooth presses and inaccurate locks"; veteran players are mainly worried about strong support and excessively wide lenses weakening the upper limit of operation, dungeon tension and professional weaknesses.

The appeal of the combat level has been recognized by users, especially the new version of 3C, 3v3 collaboration, skill actions and professional division of labor; however, the complete closed loop of search, attack and retreat has not yet been verified under the new 3C framework, and still needs to be confirmed through internal competitions adjusted based on FGD feedback, and subsequent tests with veteran players.

3Comparison between new players and veteran players

Summary: The core motivations of new players are good-looking themes, easy to try, being able to team up with friends, attractive character appearance and skills; the core motivations of veteran players are legacy version memory, operation limit, dungeon tension, professional weaknesses, equipment/building depth and community gameplay.

Main obstacles: New players are stuck and cannot understand the target, there are too many and small buttons, skills and enemy locking rules are unclear, and friend/enemy/HP/map information is confusing; veteran players are worried about the camera being too wide, strong auxiliary weakening technology, universal weapon block weakening professional identity, and long-range professions being too safe.
