2核心结论Core Conclusions

当前主要问题不是“玩法完全没有策略”，而是“策略没有被玩家看懂”。Deck 中设计了竞价、选角、三人队伍构筑、体力、士气、药水补偿和五回合战斗，但试玩中玩家多次追问金币、hold、stamina、tribe、胜负条件和药水用法。说明规则链条存在，但第一次体验时解释不够。

The main issue is not that the game has no strategy. The issue is that players often cannot read the strategy. The deck defines bidding, gladiator picking, team building, stamina, morale, potions, and five combat rounds, but players repeatedly asked about gold, hold, stamina, tribe, win condition, and potion usage during the playtest.

战斗阶段的归因感不足。自动战斗本身可以成立，但玩家需要知道“为什么攻击这个人”“为什么击杀算别人”“为什么我突然被集火”。目前讨论中出现了“我不能做任何事吗”“为什么突然打我”“最后击杀不是我的”等反馈，说明战斗结果还没有被清楚解释。

Combat attribution is weak. Auto-battle can work as a format, but players need to understand why a unit targets someone, why a kill is credited to another player, and why they are suddenly focused. The transcript includes reactions such as “So I can do nothing during the battle?”, “Why did you suddenly beat me?”, and “In the end, the kill is not mine.”

正向信号存在，但还没有稳定转化为清晰乐趣。有人觉得多人一起玩更有意思，也有人开始用金币、星级和本回合投入来讨论策略；同时也有人直接说“不知道在玩什么”“不觉得战斗有策略”。这意味着需要先解决信息表达和复盘，再继续判断玩法深度。

There are positive signals, but they have not yet become a stable, readable source of fun. Some players found multiplayer more interesting, and some started reasoning about money, star level, and round investment. At the same time, others said they did not know what they were playing or did not see strategy in combat.

稳定性和节奏问题会干扰玩法判断。讨论中出现 Samsung 上 UI broken、断线后无法重连、各端战斗结束时间不同、输入麻烦和等待时间长等问题。后续如果要判断玩法本身，最好先减少这些基础体验干扰。

Stability and pacing issues interfere with gameplay judgment. The discussion mentions broken UI on a Samsung phone, no reconnection support, different battle completion timing across devices, annoying input, and long waiting. These should be controlled before making stronger calls about the core gameplay.
